# First-run questions

Ask these once, as one list, on a first page. The caregiver can skip any question, and a skipped answer shows as "not given". Make the page with the person's input where you can (see [sources](sources.md)).

About them now:
1. What name do they answer to? Any other names, such as a maiden name or a nickname?
2. How old are they? Height and build?
3. Hair: colour and length?
4. Glasses, hearing aids, and what they usually wear or carry?
5. How do they communicate? For example, a language they understand best.
6. Conditions police should know, in your words, including medicines they need.

Who they were (people with dementia can go back in time):
7. What work did they used to do?
8. Where did they used to live?
9. Where did they used to go, such as a church or a club?
10. Have they been talking about going back somewhere?

Places and safety:
11. Where do they walk to now?
12. Dangerous places nearby, such as busy roads, water or woods?
13. What helps them feel safe when someone approaches, and what upsets or scares them, like loud noises or being touched? Have they had a bad experience with police or people in uniform? (yes / no)
14. A search-and-rescue volunteer suggests: talk softly, be patient, look them in the eye, move away from crowds, keep it calm and quiet, and go along with what they say. Keep or drop each for this person?
15. Who should police call? Name, relationship and phone number.

About this page:
16. Have they been told about this page, and did they have a say in it?
17. What is the person album called in UGREEN Photos? If there is none, name a photo folder.
18. Do you keep a notes file about them? If so, which file? (optional)
19. Your name, for the line saying who confirmed the page.
