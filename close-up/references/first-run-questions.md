# First-run questions

Ask these once, as one list, on a first page. The caregiver can skip any question. An unanswered question counts as skipped. Make the page with the person's input where you can (see [sources](sources.md)).

About them now:
1. What name do they answer to? Any other names, such as a maiden name or a nickname? Their full name and date of birth?
2. How old are they? Height and build? Right- or left-handed?
3. Hair: colour and length?
4. Glasses, hearing aids, and what they usually wear or carry?
5. How do they communicate? For example, a language they understand best.
6. Conditions police should know, most dangerous first, in your words, including medicines they need. Photos of the medicine labels? Name the image files, or say none.

Who they were (people with dementia can go back in time):
7. What work did they used to do?
8. Where did they used to live?
9. Where did they used to go, such as a church or a club?
10. Have they been talking about going back somewhere?

Places and safety:
11. Where do they walk to now? Is there a direction they tend to head?
12. Dangerous places nearby, such as busy roads, water or woods?
13. What helps them feel safe when someone approaches, and what upsets or scares them, like loud noises or being touched? Have they had a bad experience with police or people in uniform? (yes / no)
14. A search-and-rescue volunteer suggests: talk softly, be patient, look them in the eye, move away from crowds, keep it calm and quiet, and go along with what they say. Keep or drop each for this person?
15. Who should police call? Name, relationship and phone number. Are they in a tracking or registry program, such as a police registry or a bracelet program like Project Lifesaver? Which one? (the name only, no ID numbers)

About this page:
16. Have they been told about this page, and did they have a say in it?
17. What is the person album called in UGREEN Photos? If there is none, name a photo folder.
18. Do you keep a notes file about them? If so, which file? (optional)
19. Your name, for the line saying who confirmed the page.

For police (optional):
20. Do they drive? The car's year, make, model, colour and plate.
21. The phone they carry, its number and carrier, and any tracker and where it is kept, such as a shoe or a purse.
22. Have they gone missing before? Where were they found, and when?
