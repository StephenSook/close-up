# Disclosures

## AI tools and what they did

- Claude Code (Anthropic Claude models): research, idea generation and comparison, the name Close-Up, first drafts of the proposal answers (chosen and edited by Stephen Sookra), drafts of the Devpost write-up (read and rewritten by Stephen Sookra), drafting this Skill and its references, the made-up test cases, review, and the dry run, where a Claude Haiku model played the Uliya parent.
- Codex (OpenAI models through the Codex CLI): idea generation, adversarial review, the made-up test world and expert replies for the dry run (written without reading this package), a line-for-line port of UGREEN's Skill checker that we run locally to check this package, and the layouts of the Devpost gallery images (HTML rendered to PNG, made-up details labelled SAMPLE).
- Grok (xAI): idea generation, and review of this package, the proposal answers and the write-up.
- Greptile: automated review of the package repository's pull request.
- Firecrawl, Exa, Tavily, Perplexity, Brave Search, Apify and TinyFish: searching and reading public web pages and posts during research, including the guidance pages quoted in [sources](sources.md).
- Whisper (OpenAI speech models, run on our own computer): transcribing the recorded interview quoted in [sources](sources.md), with permission.

## Third-party components

None. The package is Markdown only. It quotes three public guidance pages and one interview, listed in [sources](sources.md).

## Built during the hackathon

All of it, written for this hackathon. Work began on 2026-11-11.

## Test data and stand-ins

No real person with dementia, no real caregiver and no real care papers were used. Test notes and expert replies are made up and labelled as made up. In photo tests the person is played by two consenting adults, the entrant and one other, labelled as stand-ins wherever they appear.
