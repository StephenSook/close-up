## Alzheimer's Association, Wandering

https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering

- "Keep a recent, close-up photo of the person on hand to give to police, should the need arise."
- "If the person is not found within 15 minutes, call 911 to file a missing person's report. Inform the authorities that the person has dementia."
- "Create a list of places the person might wander to, such as past jobs, former homes, places of worship or a favorite restaurant."
- "Identify potentially dangerous areas near the home, such as bodies of water, open stairwells, dense foliage, tunnels, bus stops and roads with heavy traffic."

Used for: "say they have dementia" in M19, questions 8, 9 and 12. The 15-minute line is US guidance, not used as a wait.

## Alzheimer's Foundation of America, Dementia and Wandering: Reducing the Risks

https://alzfdn.org/wandering-and-demenita/ (page modified 2024-01-02)

- "With input from the person to the greatest extent possible"
- "Identify dangerous areas" (the page continues: "such as busy roads, bodies of water and wooded areas")
- "Inform first responders and 911 operators that the person has dementia and provide any information they can use to help the person feel safer when approached"

Used for: questions 12, 13 and 16 and their page lines.

## Dementia UK, What is the Herbert Protocol for dementia?

https://www.dementiauk.org/information-and-support/living-with-dementia/herbert-protocol/ (page modified 2026-10-20)

- "Review the Herbert Protocol form every six months, or after any significant changes, for example if the person has a change of address or new medical needs"
- "Do not delay contacting the police while you search."
- "a recent photograph and what they are wearing, if known"
- "information that may help someone approach, reassure and communicate with the person"

Used for: the six-month refresh-by date, M19, the clothing question in E1, questions 13 and 14.

## A volunteer search-and-rescue leader, interviewed by phone on 2026-11-11

Quoted with his permission (he is named in our proposal). Quotes are word for word from the call recording.

- "Don't look for them first. Call the police first."
- "The photo needs to be as fresh as possible."
- "Don't give me a 25-year-old photo. It's not gonna help me."
- "You have to know who that person was, not who they are now."

Paraphrased from the same call:
- Ask other names, past work, past homes, places they used to go, and places they talk about going back to.
- A photo in the clothes they wear today is best.
- Find out where the cameras are first: a doorbell camera can show which way the person went.
- A person with bad experiences of police may run from a uniform. Talk softly, be patient, look them in the eye, move away from crowds, keep it calm and quiet, and go along with what they say.
- Update the page as their condition changes.

Used for: M19, questions 1, 7 to 10, 13 and 14, E1 to E3, and the refresh wording.
